Key patterns

Do not memorize 24 chords.
Remember two words: CFG and iDEA.

Every major or minor triad on the piano is a pattern of white and black keys. There are four patterns. The trio that holds the chord letter tells you which one.

CFG

The three main chords of C major — thousands of songs stand on them. Major = all white.

iDEA

Hidden in the word idea. Minor = all white. Major has a black key in the middle.

1 Skeleton — skip one letter

Take the chord letter, skip one, take the next. On the white keys that is “every other one”.

2 Pattern — which keys turn black

The pattern says which skeleton note moves to the next black key. Take the pattern from the sheet below.

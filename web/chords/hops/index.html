Hops

A chord is two hops along the keys.

Count every key in a row — white and black. Major: hop 4, then 3. Minor is the other way: 3, then 4. It works from any key. Nothing to memorize.

① Press any key — that is the root. ② Watch the hops get counted.

C → E is 4 steps: black keys count too. Without them the rule breaks.

Frame = 7

Major and minor share the outer notes: 4+3 = 3+4 = 7. The frame stays. Only the middle moves.

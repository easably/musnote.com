Chord clock

One shape — twelve chords.

Lay the 12 keys of the octave around a circle, like hours on a clock. Any major chord is the same triangle. Turn it and you get a new chord. Minor is that triangle with the middle corner one hour back.

C
Major: sides 4 · 3 · 5 hours. The shape never changes.
Minor: 3 · 4 · 5 — the same triangle in a mirror.
1 hour on the circle = 1 key. Click any note, or press ← →.
rootthirdfifthseventhcorner colors on the clock = key colors
The same shape works for other chords too: a seventh is the quadrilateral 4·3·3·2.

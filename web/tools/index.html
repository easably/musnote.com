Voice performance tools for languages & career

Honest practice tools: turn speech or song into text, hear slow clear pronunciation, find your comfortable octave, and shadow a phrase while watching pitch — not party karaoke.

STT

Audio / song → text

Upload a clip or paste an audio URL. Get a transcript with timestamps — ideal for lyrics study and shadowing scripts.

 TTS

Slow pronunciation

Paste any phrase. Hear clear, slowed speech so you can match rhythm and intonation for English practice.

 Pitch

Find my octave

Sing or speak into the mic to measure your range, then preview a reference clip shifted toward your octave.

 Shadowing

Shadow a phrase

Generate a model line, then speak along. Live pitch readout helps you match the melody of the phrase.

What MusNote optimizes for

Is this accent reduction?

We start with intonation and rhythm — the melody of speech. That is the honest first step toward clearer pronunciation. Full phoneme accent coaching comes later.

Who is this for?

Language learners who shadow native audio, and professionals who want a more confident speaking voice for interviews and presentations.

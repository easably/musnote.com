What the iOS app is

MusNote Voice (working name) is the mobile home for our live pitch stack — the same idea as the web voice tools, built for the mic in your pocket.

  • Live pitch graph — watch your contour while you speak or sing exercises.
  • Note + cents gauge — fine-tune intonation in real time.
  • Record & replay — hear yourself back after a short take.
  • On-device — pitch runs on the phone; nothing is required from a server for the core mirror.

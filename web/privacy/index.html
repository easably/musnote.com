1. Who we are and what this covers

The company named above is the controller of the personal data described here. In this policy we call it “we”, “us” or “the Company”, and we call you “you”.

This policy covers the products we publish under the MusNote name: the website musnote.com and the MusNote mobile app.

It does not cover other companies’ websites or apps that we link to. When you follow a link out, that destination’s privacy policy applies.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The MusNote website does not use cookies. It does not load a visit counter or an advertising tag. The only value stored in the browser is the language you chose, in localStorage. That is not a cookie, and it is not sent to us.

There is no cookie banner, because there is nothing optional to accept.

6. Sending data outside the EEA

Note-reading runs on our own servers. The website is delivered by Cloudflare, and the voice tools call FreeDubbing. Those providers may process data outside the European Economic Area.

Where a transfer needs a safeguard, it relies on a lawful mechanism, such as an adequacy decision of the European Commission or Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask which mechanism covers a given provider, and we will tell you.

7. How long we keep data

The retention column in section 3 is the rule.

Audio you submit for notes is deleted as soon as the response is sent. The file is removed in the same request, including when the request fails.

Support mail is kept for 24 months after the request is closed.

Technical logs: the notes service writes a log line for each request. That line contains the private network address of our own proxy, not your public IP address. Docker stores that log in files of at most 10 MB and keeps only the three newest files. When a file fills up, the oldest file is deleted. On 21 November 2026 the notes log was about 40 KB after 15 hours, so at that rate the cap holds many months of lines, not a few days. The website does not write its own access log. Cloudflare, as the host, may keep connection data under its own policy.

We may keep a particular record longer if we need it for a legal claim or a legal duty. That does not apply to audio we have already deleted.

10. If you are elsewhere

If your local law gives you more than this policy does, your local law wins.

11. Children

MusNote is not directed at children under 16. We do not create accounts.

If you believe a child has sent us personal data — a recording, a file, or a letter — write to support@englishingames.com and we will delete it.

12. The app, and the microphone

Recordings and the notes we return are stored on the phone. Deleting a recording deletes its file from the phone. There are no purchases in the app.

The microphone is used only while you are recording, and only after you press the record button. The recording is sent to our servers only if you then ask for notes. Until you do that, it stays on the phone.

13. How we protect data

We use measures appropriate to the risk. The site and the notes address are served over HTTPS. Audio submitted for notes is deleted when the response is sent. There is no account database to breach.

No system is perfectly secure. What we commit to is proportionate protection and honest handling when something goes wrong.

14. If there is a data breach

If personal data is breached and the breach is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we notify UODO within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. If the risk to you is high, we also tell you directly and without undue delay, in plain language: what happened, what data was involved, what we are doing, and what you should do.

If you think you have found a security vulnerability, please write to support@englishingames.com before disclosing it publicly. We will not pursue anyone who reports a genuine issue in good faith and gives us a reasonable chance to fix it.

15. Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions about you by purely automated means that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects. Reading notes from a file is a calculation you asked for. It does not score you, rank you, or decide anything about you.

16. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when what we do changes. The date at the top is the date of the current version.

If a change materially affects how we use your data, we say so on this page before it takes effect. Where the change needs your consent, we ask for it rather than assuming it. We do not make material changes retroactive.

Earlier versions are available on request.

17. Contact

Write to support@englishingames.com for anything in this policy. The company address and phone are on elang.app/contacts. A person reads the mail.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, because our processing does not meet the threshold in Article 37 of the GDPR. The contact above reaches the people responsible for data protection.
